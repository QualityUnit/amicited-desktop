    4. What should you look for when choosing a CRM?

    Before investing in CRM software, consider these seven factors:

    1. Ease of use: Your employees should be able to use it without extensive training.
    2. Contact management: Store customer details, communication history and important notes.
    3. Sales pipeline: Track leads from the first interaction to the final sale.
    4. Automation: Automate repetitive tasks, reminders and follow-up emails.
    5. Reporting: Understand sales performance and customer acquisition.
    6. Integrations: Connect with Gmail, Outlook, accounting software and other business tools.
    7. Scalability: Make sure the CRM can accommodate additional employees and customers.

    One important consideration: don't compare subscription prices alone. Check the cost of the features you actually need, additional users, automation limits and potential onboarding fees.
