    2. Detailed platform comparison

    1. — Best overall for ecommerce

    Website: Klaviyo

    Klaviyo is my top recommendation for established Shopify and DTC brands that want to make email a significant revenue channel.

    Main features:

    • Advanced customer segmentation based on purchase behaviour.
    • Abandoned cart and checkout automation.
    • Browse abandonment flows.
    • Post-purchase and replenishment campaigns.
    • Predictive analytics and customer lifetime value.
    • Product recommendations.
    • Email and SMS marketing.

    Pricing:

    • Free: Up to 250 active profiles and 500 emails/month.
    • 500 profiles: $20/month.
    • 1,000 profiles: $30/month.
    • 5,000 profiles: $100/month.
    • 10,000 profiles: $150/month.

    Advantages:

    • Excellent Shopify integration.
    • Very powerful automation and segmentation.
    • Detailed revenue attribution.
    • Excellent for scaling retention marketing.

    Disadvantages:

    • Can become expensive as your contact database grows.
    • Takes time to master.
    • SMS and additional functionality can increase costs.

    Verdict: Best for brands serious about scaling ecommerce revenue through personalised email marketing.

    3. — Best for DTC brands

    Website: Drip

    Drip is specifically designed around ecommerce customer journeys and revenue generation.

    Main features:

    • Behaviour-based email automation.
    • Advanced customer segmentation.
    • Abandoned cart recovery.
    • Post-purchase automation.
    • Customer lifecycle marketing.
    • Revenue attribution.
    • Personalised campaigns.

    Pricing:

    • Starts at $39/month for up to 2,500 people.
    • 14-day free trial.
    • Unlimited email sends on paid plans.

    Advantages:

    • Ecommerce-focused functionality.
    • Strong behavioural automation.
    • Good customer journey builder.
    • Straightforward pricing structure.

    Disadvantages:

    • More expensive than some alternatives for smaller stores.
    • No permanent free plan.
    • No native SMS marketing offering comparable to Klaviyo or Omnisend.

    Verdict: A strong choice for DTC brands focused on repeat purchases, customer retention and lifecycle marketing.

    4. — Best for Shopify beginners

    Website: Shopify Messaging

    Shopify's native email marketing solution is ideal if you want something simple without paying for an additional platform.

    Main features:

    • Email campaigns directly from Shopify.
    • Customer segmentation.
    • Marketing automation.
    • Product integration.
    • Campaign performance reporting.
    • Ready-made templates.

    Pricing:

    • 10,000 emails/month included with eligible Shopify plans.
    • Additional emails cost $1 per 1,000.

    Advantages:

    • No separate marketing platform subscription.
    • Direct Shopify integration.
    • Easy to use.
    • Good for basic promotional campaigns.

    Disadvantages:

    • Less sophisticated automation.
    • Less advanced segmentation.
    • Limited compared with specialist ecommerce platforms.

    Verdict: Excellent starting point, but I'd consider upgrading to Klaviyo or Omnisend as your marketing becomes more sophisticated.

    5. — Best for advanced automation

    Website: ActiveCampaign

    • Conditional email sequences.

    Verdict: Best for businesses with complex customer journeys and automation requirements.

    Here is a more useful comparison using approximately 1,000 and 5,000 contacts.

    Shopify Messaging Free within allowance Free within allowance

    Prices are monthly USD list prices, excluding taxes, promotions and additional messaging charges. The contact-based figures use a November 2026 pricing comparison.

    Important pricing differences:

    • Klaviyo charges based on active profiles.
    • Omnisend uses contact-based pricing, with additional billing considerations for automated messages.
    • Mailchimp charges according to contact tiers and plan limits.
    • Brevo primarily charges according to email sending volume.
    • Shopify Messaging charges according to emails sent beyond its free allowance.

    This distinction matters enormously. A brand with 20,000 subscribers sending only two campaigns per month might find a send-volume-based platform more economical.

    Bottom line

    My recommendation is to shortlist Klaviyo and Omnisend.

    They are particularly compelling because they are designed around ecommerce customer behaviour rather than simply sending newsletters.

    • Choose Klaviyo if you want advanced segmentation, sophisticated personalisation and detailed revenue analytics.
    • Choose Omnisend if you want excellent ecommerce automation at a more affordable price.
    • Choose Drip if your priority is retention and repeat purchases.

    I wouldn't choose purely based on the monthly subscription. The more important question is how much revenue the platform can help you generate through automated email campaigns.

    A good welcome flow, abandoned checkout sequence, post-purchase flow and win-back campaign can be considerably more valuable than saving $20–30 per month on software.
