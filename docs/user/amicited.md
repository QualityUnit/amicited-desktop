# AmICited Desktop

## Connect AmICited

Your local marketing tools work without an account. After your first result,
choose Connect AmICited or Start 7-day free trial to sign in or create an
account in your browser. Return to the app after approving your workspace.
You can cancel while the app waits. You can also connect a workspace key from
AmICited settings.

Connecting gives access to the free AmICited tools. A trial or eligible paid
plan unlocks the full set. If a tool needs that access, its result offers
Start 7-day free trial. Your connection and free tools stay available.
Disconnect in Settings whenever you want.
